# Early Visual System

## Direction

Early is a cinematic cultural archive where small online moments become durable proof. The landing page combines tactile media fragments, precise proof signals, and restrained botanical framing. It should feel authored and alive, never like a generic Web3 dashboard.

## Color

- Ink black: `#070A08`, the primary full-bleed surface.
- Mineral white: `#F1EFE7`, the primary text and receipt material.
- Electric signal: `#75FF82`, reserved for live states, proof paths, focus, and primary commands.
- Coral: `#FF5C55`, for replies and human reaction.
- Cobalt: `#315CFF`, for video and platform fragments.
- Sunflower: `#F4C84A`, for music and attention moments.
- Cyan: `#52D7DD`, for timestamps and authenticated data.

Never use purple or decorative gradients. Rich secondary colors belong to cultural-media artifacts; they do not become competing page backgrounds.

## Typography

Use the local Geist family for reliable loading. Display copy is large, wide-set, and direct with no negative tracking. Body copy remains human, readable, and limited to comfortable measures. Monospace is reserved for timestamps, hashes, protocol labels, and code.

## Layout

The landing page uses a fluid twelve-column composition with full-bleed bands and a constrained inner canvas. The hero remains asymmetric: brand promise and working X input on the left, living archive on the right. Every first viewport reveals a hint of the next section. Section rhythm alternates between immersive single ideas and denser proof/infrastructure moments.

Cards are used only for real artifacts such as posts, comments, receipts, and code examples. Page sections and audience narratives remain unframed. Corners stay at or below `8px` except circular status marks.

## Motifs

- A small radial signal mark identifies Early and verified transitions.
- Thin electric-green paths connect source activity to proof.
- Posts, comments, timestamps, progress marks, and receipts behave like physical cultural artifacts.
- Wind-swept white flowers enter from selected corners and move slowly. They frame the archive without becoming its subject.
- Attention is visualized as expanding fields of small points, not vanity-metric dashboards.

## Motion

The first load reveals the headline, proof form, and archive in a single sequence. Scroll motion transforms one small post into an attention field, then carries the proof into a private history. Ambient artifact and flower motion is slow and bounded. Functional feedback is immediate. Reduced-motion mode preserves all information and removes continuous movement.

## Components

- Landing navigation: transparent, full-width, 44px minimum targets.
- Proof entry: visible label, URL input, action button, inline error state.
- Cultural artifact: sharp paper or ink material, small radius, real semantic content.
- Receipt: mineral paper, perforated edge treatment, verification state.
- FAQ: accessible accordion with clear expanded state; final content section.
- Proof application states: retain the existing loading and verified flows while progressively adopting these tokens.

## Responsive Behavior

Desktop uses the full archive composition. Tablet stacks copy above a shallower archive while keeping the proof form visible. Mobile simplifies artifact depth, hides nonessential decoration, keeps 44px controls, and turns audience/platform sequences into readable vertical narratives rather than scaled-down desktop scenes.
