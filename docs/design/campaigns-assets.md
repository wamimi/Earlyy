## Generation direction

Concert: Premium candid editorial photograph of a concert audience, diverse young adults seen from behind, raised hands, amber stage haze and bokeh, natural photographic detail. Portrait composition for a tall catalogue card. No text, branding, or UI.

Merchandise: Premium product editorial photograph of a charcoal cotton band T-shirt on an independent record-store table, with a folded shirt and vinyl behind it. Cream and burnt-orange abstract screen print, warm directional daylight, realistic fabric. Wide composition. No readable lettering, logos, or UI.

Records: Editorial close-up of a hand browsing vintage vinyl at a real independent record shop. Warm amber light with green tones, shallow depth of field, natural materials. Wide composition. No added text or UI.

Tickets: Premium candid editorial photograph of friends’ hands holding cream concert passes in front of warm stage lighting. Portrait composition. The passes read LIVE MUSIC and ALL ACCESS, with a barcode. No other lettering, branding, or UI.

## Interaction scope

Category controls switch the opportunity catalogue and featured benefit. Each card opens a details dialog. The campaign planner downloads a local Markdown brief; it does not submit data or publish a campaign. A single availability note appears in opportunity details, where a visitor would otherwise expect to claim an offer.
