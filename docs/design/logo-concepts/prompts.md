## first-signal

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished professional logo proposal presentation for Early, a cultural participation and early-supporter registry connecting fans, creators, communities and brands with verifiable recognition, access and rewards. Direction First Signal. A distinctive proprietary lowercase-e inspired symbol built from three rounded horizontal signal strokes, with a single coral leading stroke displaced slightly toward the left. The silhouette must feel like an intentional compact E monogram and a first beat that others follow, NOT a WiFi icon, generic bar chart, audio equalizer, leaf or speed lines. Bold simple geometry, beautifully balanced negative space. Emerald dominant, coral only one initiating element.
 Exact wordmark: "Early" (E capital followed by lowercase a r l y). Draw a custom refined friendly geometric sans-serif wordmark, medium-bold, lightly rounded, open counters, tight optically balanced spacing, similar in warmth to Plus Jakarta Sans but crafted, no stock-looking typography.
 Palette emerald #123F32, coral #FF785F, warm ivory #FFF8EE. Flat vector-like crisp artwork only, no gradients, shadows, 3D or lighting. On a clean warm ivory landscape branding sheet, display a large main horizontal symbol-plus-Early lockup across the upper two thirds. In the lower third show three small consistent practical demonstrations: emerald-only symbol on ivory, ivory reversed lockup on an emerald rectangular swatch, and the symbol centred in a simple app icon thumbnail. All demonstrations use EXACTLY the same symbol geometry. A tiny discrete heading at upper left says "First Signal". Restrained premium brand studio presentation, generous whitespace. No extra slogans, no invented company names, no nature imagery. Symbol must remain readable at favicon size, accessible and reproducible in one colour. This is one distinct logo direction, not a collection of different marks.
