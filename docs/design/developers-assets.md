## Loyalty prompt
Premium candid editorial photograph for a cultural loyalty infrastructure website, wide landscape 2:1. A young adult woman with east Asian features wearing a dark emerald green casual top, seated in a warm independent coffee and record shop, smiling naturally while checking her smartphone. Authentic unposed expression, tactile interior, mint and deep green surroundings, daylight with subtle warm copper highlights, shallow depth of field. Face left of frame, right third relatively quiet for a small HTML status overlay. No text, no logos, no website interface, no screen UI.
